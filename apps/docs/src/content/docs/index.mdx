## How it works

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="1. Mandate Vault" icon="approve-check">
    An agent trades from a vault that knows its rules: which tokens, which contracts, where funds
    may go, how much per trade and per day. A breach is blocked or recorded on chain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="2. The Arena" icon="warning">
    AI attacker scenarios across 10 classes are planted in the agent's world inside a sandbox. The
    verdict of each episode is the vault's breach log.
  </Card>
  <Card title="3. Bonded Score" icon="star">
    Breach rates become an expected loss, a premium and a score from 0 to 100, signed by the
    attester and readable on Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Ethereum and Solana.
  </Card>
  <Card title="4. Bond and cover" icon="open-book">
    The operator's bond pays first. Underwriters fund a pool that pays the rest. Depositors buy
    cover priced from the score.
  </Card>
  <Card title="5. Automatic claims" icon="rocket">
    When the vault records a breach, the keeper files the claim within seconds. The contract slashes
    the bond, then the pool pays.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sentinel" icon="setting">
    An optional real-time guard that flags manipulated actions before they reach the vault. It is
    advisory: payouts never depend on it.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Payouts and pass or fail are decided by deterministic logic in the vault and cover contracts,
never by an AI.
